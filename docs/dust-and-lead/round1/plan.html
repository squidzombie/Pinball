What I changed from the sketch

  • Gold Pan moved left and made smaller, about 13 cm, or 4½ balls, across. Where the sketch has it, the right flipper's shot to the Gold Mine ramp goes straight through the pan.
  • Railroad lane entrance moved up to about halfway up the table. Down at the bottom left, neither flipper can reach it.
  • Jailhouse and Stagecoach entrances turned to face the flipper that shoots them. Facing straight down, their side walls stopped most shots.
  • Saloon lane entrance moved down and the Stagecoach moved toward the middle. The left flipper's sharpest shots to the right run along the right wall, and the Stagecoach was sitting in their path.
  • The Bank and General Store are artwork, not targets. The Gold Mine shot runs right where they'd stand. The Bank could frame the mine ramp's entrance.
  • The plunge and both orbits go to the saloon. That gives the saloon flipper plenty to do, as your sketch asks ("accessible, repeatable shot").

What moves, so it isn't plain

Cyber Psychosis is mostly flat targets. This one is built around toys that move, and the code draws all of them:

  • The minecart rides up the elevator with the ball in it.
  • The outlaw walks from window to window, and the clock runs down to high noon.
  • The saloon doors swing every time a ball comes through.
  • The stagecoach rolls along its ramp alongside the ball.
  • Locked balls wait in the jail cell, and the bars blow out for the jailbreak.
  • The ball clatters through the Gold Pan's pins, and its cup lights up.
  • A train runs up the Railroad lane during Train Robbery.
  • The barrels rock and kick up dust when hit.
  • Lit windows and lanterns in every building.
  • Three levels: the orbits on the floor, the Stagecoach ramp above them, and the mine's wire track above that. That's more depth than Cyber Psychosis has anywhere.
