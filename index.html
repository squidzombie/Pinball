TAP OR PRESS ENTER TO PLAY

Flippers
Z / ← / left Shift  ·  / / → / right Shift  ·  or tap the left / right half
Plunger
hold Space or ↓, release to launch  ·  or hold the right half
Other
X nudge  ·  M mute  ·  P pause  ·  V flat / tilted view  ·  the % button sets the game speed
  • B-R-E-A-C-H: hit the six red targets beside the Breach (the glowing scoop), any order, to light a job. Then shoot the Breach to start it.
  • Reactor: knock down its three drop targets to light the lock, then shoot the Skyway ramp to lock a ball. Three locks start a multiball.
  • R-A-G-E (bottom lanes) and CAM 1-2-3 (top lanes): roll through a lane to light it. The flipper buttons slide the lit lights. Light them all for a bonus.
  • The vault (top left): hit its tumblers in the order of the code on the display for a multiball.
