  • B-R-E-A-C-H: hit the six red targets by the Breach (the glowing scoop) to break in, then shoot the Breach to start Infiltrate. Spell it again for a jackpot there (the third time, an extra ball too).
  • Next jobs: after Infiltrate, cameras down (all three CAM lanes) lights Hack or Route; after one of those, the Reactor's drop targets light Payload or Extract. Jobs carry on if you lose the ball.
  • Reactor: its drop targets also light the lock: shoot the Skyway to lock a ball. Three locks start a multiball.
  • R-A-G-E (bottom lanes) and CAM 1-2-3 (top lanes): roll through a lane to light it; the flippers slide the lit lights. Finish R-A-G-E on a gutter lane to save that ball.
  • The vault (top left): hit its tumblers in the order on the display for a multiball.
